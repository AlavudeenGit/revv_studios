About Revv Studios

An AI-first studio built for brands that refuse to look ordinary.

We're a small, hands-on team based in Chennai, working with clients across India, the UAE, and the GCC region.

Our Story

From a two-person freelance shop to a full-service studio.

Revv Studios started as a freelance operation producing AI content for a handful of early clients. What began as short video production quickly grew into full campaigns — and then into complete digital builds, as clients kept asking us to solve problems well beyond the original brief.

Today, we sit at the intersection of creative direction, data-driven marketing, and applied AI. We don't bolt AI onto old workflows — we design campaigns, videos, and websites around what's possible today, for recruitment agencies, facility management platforms, coaching brands, and fast-growing SMEs across India and the Gulf.
